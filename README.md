> **RetailPulse** is an enterprise-grade retail business intelligence and analytics solution developed in Microsoft Power BI. It transforms raw transactional, customer, inventory, and geographic retail data into strategic, actionable decision-making insights across sales performance, customer lifetime value, RFM segmentation, and supply chain health.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Modern retail organizations operate across multi-channel environments where monitoring sales velocity, customer churn, regional demand, and return liabilities in real-time is vital. **RetailPulse** bridges data silos by providing an end-to-end interactive cockpit for retail executives, category managers, supply chain teams, and marketing analysts.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Architecture & Page Breakdown

The `Retailpulse.pbix` workbook comprises interactive reporting modules structured into strategic domains:

```
RetailPulse Power BI Suite
├── 📈 Executive Dashboards
│   ├── Page 1 - 3: High-Level Executive Summary & Strategic Cockpit
│   └── Orders & Transactions: Transactional Flow & Basket Dynamics
├── 👥 Customer Intelligence
│   ├── Customer Analysis: Demographics, CLV, & Cohorts
│   └── Customer Behaviour & RFM: Behavioral Tiering (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)
├── 🌍 Geographic & Seasonal Trends
│   ├── Country & Region Sales: Multi-regional Sales & Expansion Mapping
│   └── Monthly & Seasonal Sales: Seasonality, MoM/YoY Trends & Peaks
├── 📦 Operations & Financials
│   ├── Revenue & Returns: Margin Health, Refund Rates & Return Impact
│   └── Inventory & Demand: Stock Velocity, Depletion Risk & Reorder Points
└── 🔬 Advanced & Exploratory Analytics
    ├── Advanced Analytics: Key Drivers, Correlations & Decomposition Trees
    └── Interactive Analysis: Ad-hoc Drill-down & Multi-parameter Slicing
```

### 1. 📈 Executive Cockpit & KPI Overviews (`Pages 1–3`)
- **Core Metrics:** Total Gross/Net Revenue, Total Order Volume, Units Sold, Average Order Value (AOV), and Overall Return Rate.
- **Trend Lines:** High-level performance trajectories comparing current targets against historical baselines.
- **Top Contributors:** Quick-view snapshots of top revenue-driving product categories and leading territories.

### 2. 👥 Customer Analysis
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):** Evaluation of long-term customer worth across acquisition channels.
- **New vs. Returning Dynamics:** Customer retention curves, repeat purchase ratios, and loyalty progression.
- **Demographic Segmentation:** Distribution of buyers across age brackets, gender, and customer tiers.

### 3. 🌍 Country & Region Sales
- **Geographic Mapping:** Interactive spatial maps and choropleths highlighting revenue intensity across global and regional markets.
- **Territory Comparison:** Cross-regional benchmark analysis assessing market penetration and localized growth rates.
- **Regional Product Preferences:** Identifying which product lines outperform in specific geographical clusters.

### 4. 📅 Monthly & Seasonal Sales
- **Temporal Analysis:** Month-over-Month (MoM) and Year-over-Year (YoY) revenue and order tracking.
- **Seasonality & Holiday Spikes:** Uncovering surge periods (Q4 holiday rush, summer promos, seasonal demand fluctuations).
- **Day-of-Week & Intraday Patterns:** Pinpointing peak shopping days to optimize staffing, promotion launches, and inventory staging.

### 5. 🎯 Customer Behaviour & RFM Segmentation
- **RFM Matrix Calculation:**
  - **Recency ($R$):** Days since the customer's last purchase.
  - **Frequency ($F$):** Total count of transactions completed.
  - **Monetary ($M$):** Cumulative revenue generated by the customer.
- **Customer Behavioral Clusters:**
  - 🏆 *Champions / VIPs:* High frequency, high spend, recent activity — priority for loyalty perks.
  - 💎 *Loyal Customers:* Consistent purchasing patterns — targets for upselling/cross-selling.
  - ⚠️ *At Risk / Churn Candidates:* High historical spend but extended inactivity — candidates for win-back campaigns.
  - 💤 *Lost / Hibernating:* Low frequency and stale engagement.

### 6. 💰 Revenue & Returns Analysis
- **Gross vs. Net Realization:** Impact of discounts, vouchers, and product refunds on bottom-line margins.
- **Return Rate Analytics:** Identification of products and categories with disproportionately high return rates.
- **Root Cause & Reason Breakdown:** Financial loss mitigation by correlating returns to defective items, sizing issues, or shipping delays.

### 7. 📦 Inventory & Demand Planning
- **Stock Turnover Ratio:** Monitoring inventory health, carrying costs, and shelf velocity.
- **Fast vs. Slow-Moving Stock:** Highlighting stagnant inventory to facilitate clearance strategies and prevent dead-stock accumulation.
- **Demand Forecasting & Reorder Alerts:** Proactive indicators alerting managers before critical stockout thresholds are reached.

### 8. 🧾 Orders & Transactions
- **Cart & Basket Dynamics:** Average units per transaction (UPT) and product bundling affinities.
- **Payment & Delivery Channels:** Breakdown of payment methods (credit, digital wallets, COD) and shipping provider transit benchmarks.
- **Fulfillment Timelines:** Order placement to dispatch and final delivery cycle times.

### 9. 🧠 Advanced Analytics & Decomposition
- **Key Influencers:** AI-assisted identification of variables that most significantly drive high purchase amounts or product returns.
- **Decomposition Tree:** Interactive root-cause breakdown drilling down from aggregate revenue into Category > Region > Customer Segment > Channel.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Automated detection of sudden sales outliers, fraudulent transactions, or unexpected demand drops.

### 10. 🔍 Interactive Self-Service Analytics
- **Dynamic Slicing:** Cross-filtering across dates, categories, shipping statuses, and customer segments.
- **Drill-Through Capabilities:** Instant transitions from macro-level enterprise metrics down to individual invoice and SKU line items.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Implementation Details

| Component | Technology | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Platform** | Microsoft Power BI Desktop | Data modeling, visualization, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboard layout |
| **Data Engine** | Power Query (M Language) | Data ingestion, schema normalization, type coercion, cleaning, and transformation |
| **Analytical Engine** | DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) | Time intelligence, dynamic measures, RFM scoring, and KPI calculations |
| **Data Model** | Star Schema | Fact tables (`Sales`, `Returns`, `Inventory`) connected to Dimensions (`Customers`, `Products`, `Calendar`, `Geography`) |

## 📈 Key DAX Measures & Metrics Sample

The report leverages advanced DAX patterns, including:

- **Time Intelligence:**
  ```dax
  Revenue YTD = TOTALYTD(SUM(Sales[TotalAmount]), 'Calendar'[Date])
  ```
  ```dax
  Revenue YoY Growth % = 
  VAR CurrentYearSales = [Total Revenue]
  VAR PriorYearSales = CALCULATE([Total Revenue], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR('Calendar'[Date]))
  RETURN
      DIVIDE(CurrentYearSales - PriorYearSales, PriorYearSales, 0)
  ```

- **Average Order Value (AOV):**
  ```dax
  Average Order Value = DIVIDE([Total Revenue], DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[OrderID]), 0)
  ```

- **Return Rate %:**
  ```dax
  Return Rate % = DIVIDE([Total Returned Quantity], [Total Units Sold], 0)
  ```

- **RFM Customer Scoring:**
  Dynamic ranking and segmentation measures classifying customer records into deciles and persona tiers.

## 🚀 Getting Started & How to Explore

### Prerequisites
- [Microsoft Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop/) (Windows 10/11, latest version recommended).

### Steps to Open and Interact
1. **Clone the Repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/sanketsutar09/RetailPulse.git
   cd RetailPulse
   ```
2. **Open the Report:**
   - Double-click `Retailpulse.pbix` to launch it directly in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. **Explore the Dashboards:**
   - Use the navigation tabs at the bottom to switch between pages.
   - Utilize slicers and filters on the top and right panes to view specific date periods, product lines, and geographical regions.
   - Hold `Ctrl` (or click) on visuals to cross-filter across elements.
